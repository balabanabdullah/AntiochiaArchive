Chronicle of a City

Twenty-Five Centuries of Heritage

Founded in 300 BCE along the Orontes River, Antioch grew into one of the ancient Mediterranean's great capitals—a crossroads of silk routes, philosophies, and faith traditions.

300 BCE — Roman Capital

Crossroads of the Ancient Mediterranean

Established by Seleucus I Nicator, Antioch became the third largest city of the Roman Empire, renowned for its colonnaded avenues and mosaic masterpieces.

Medieval & Ottoman Eras

Mosaic of Cultures & Trade

Through centuries of transformation, the city maintained its position as a vibrant hub of silk trade, craftsmanship, and peaceful co-existence.

Modern Memory

Preserving Identity Across Time

Today, AntiochiaArchive safeguards physical landmarks and intangible heritage so future generations can retrace the living memory of Antioch.
